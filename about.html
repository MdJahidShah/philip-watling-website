The Journey: From Accident to Purpose

Philip Watling’s life is defined by determination, empathy, and an unbreakable sense of humour. After graduating in Zoology and Ecology, he secured his dream job caring for horses. But in December 1994, an accident brought his heart to a halt and left him in a coma for weeks.

During his recovery, Philip faced damage to his vision and speech, yet he refused to let physical limitations curtail his zest for life. Over years of dedicated physiotherapy at Bannatyne’s Health Club and creative studies in Milton Keynes, he taught himself Russian (earning a GCSE Grade C) and rebuilt his social and emotional independence.

Giving Back to Others

Today, Philip works as a dedicated Support Worker for an autistic man, walking alongside him every week. In addition, he regularly volunteers at the local community fridge and dedicates hours to chatting with and mentoring people facing severe life challenges.

"Ultimately, I want the message of peace, humour, and friendship to pervade society. There is always hope, even when life won't be quite the same as you've always known it."
