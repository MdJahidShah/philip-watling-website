# Philip Watling - Official Author Website

This repository contains the complete, modern, production-ready static website designed for author **Philip Watling** and his autobiography ***Flight of a Lifetime: Miracles Do Happen***.

## 🌟 Key Features & Improvements
- **Modern Visual Hierarchy:** Beautiful typography pairing (Playfair Display + Inter), 3D CSS book mockup, deep navy and warm gold aesthetic.
- **Conversion-Optimized Funnel:** Direct purchase links for Amazon, Barnes & Noble, ThriftBooks, and Goodreads; interactive sample chapter reader; reader newsletter capture form.
- **Structured Content Architecture:**
  - `index.html`: High-converting author landing page with hero, book synopsis, author summary, praise, and contact.
  - `book.html`: Dedicated book specs, retailer showcase, and chapter excerpt.
  - `about.html`: Philip's inspiring biography, education at Queen Mary University, neuro-recovery, and community support work.
  - `media.html`: Video showcase (Royal London HEMS air ambulance reunion), podcast features, and downloadable press kit.
  - `reviews.html`: Reader testimonials and 5-star ratings.
  - `contact.html`: Clean contact and speaking booking form.
- **SEO & Performance Ready:** Embedded Schema.org JSON-LD markup for `Book` and `Person` entities, Open Graph meta tags, responsive mobile drawer menu, and zero external framework bloat.
