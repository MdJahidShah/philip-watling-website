About the Memoir

In Flight of a Lifetime, Philip Watling takes readers down a path few ever walk—and fewer return from. From a young university graduate working his dream job with horses, to facing sudden, life-threatening trauma and a Glasgow Coma Score of 5, Philip's journey is a beacon of hope.

Beyond the trauma, the book delves into Philip's quest to find his biological mother, Elizabeth, demonstrating how curiosity, faith in human goodness, and courage can awaken possibilities in anyone facing severe adversity.
